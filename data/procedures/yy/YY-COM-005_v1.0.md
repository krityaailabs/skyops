# YY Connect interline fare agreements

## Purpose

This document lists the commercial terms YY Connect has agreed with partner airlines for rebooking disrupted passengers. It is confidential to YY Connect.

## Rebooking on partners

| Partner | Rebooking rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ZZ | Net fare minus 35 percent | Same-day rebooking only |
| Other partners | Published fare | Duty officer approval |

## Confidentiality

Do not disclose these terms to passengers, to ZZ staff, or to any other airline.

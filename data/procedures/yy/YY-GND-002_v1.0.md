# YY Connect delay care at HUB

## Purpose

SkyOps ground staff handle YY Connect departures at HUB under contract. This procedure sets the care YY Connect passengers receive; it is not the ZZ procedure.

## Care levels

| Delay at the gate | Care offered | Who authorises |
|---|---|---|
| 3 hours or more | Meal voucher, 12 EUR per passenger | Handling agent |
| Overnight | Hotel room and transport | Handling agent up to 120 EUR, YY duty officer above |

## Hotel accommodation

1. Call the YY duty officer before booking any hotel.
2. Book from the YY contracted hotel list, never the ZZ list.
3. A hotel voucher above 120 EUR needs the YY duty officer's approval.

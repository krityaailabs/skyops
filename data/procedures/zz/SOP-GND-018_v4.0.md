# Delayed and damaged baggage

## Purpose

This procedure covers bags that do not arrive with the passenger at HUB, and bags that arrive damaged.

## Reporting at the airport

1. Ask the passenger for the bag tag and the boarding pass.
2. Raise a property irregularity report at the baggage desk and give the passenger its reference.
3. Describe the bag: type, colour, brand and any distinctive marks.
4. Record a delivery address and a contact number.

## Deadlines for written claims

| Problem | Written claim within |
|---|---|
| Damaged bag | 7 days of receiving it |
| Delayed bag | 21 days of receiving it |
| Lost bag | After 21 days, the bag is treated as lost |

## Essentials

Passengers whose bag is delayed away from home may buy essentials. Explain that receipts are needed for a claim, and that reasonable costs are reimbursed.

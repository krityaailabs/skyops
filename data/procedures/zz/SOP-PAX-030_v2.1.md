# Unaccompanied minors

## Purpose

This procedure covers children travelling without an adult on ZZ flights from HUB. A child in this service is never left without a named, trained escort.

## Age bands

| Age | Service | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Under 5 | Not accepted alone | Must travel with an adult |
| 5 to 11 | Unaccompanied minor service required | Direct flights only |
| 12 to 15 | Service optional, on request | Connections allowed with service |

## Handover at departure

1. Check the handover form: the adult dropping off, the adult collecting at the destination, and their identity documents.
2. The adult dropping off stays at the airport until the flight has departed.
3. The escort signs the form when they take the child, and again at the aircraft door when the cabin crew take over.
4. Keep the form with the flight documents.

## Delays and cancellations

If the flight is delayed for more than 2 hours or cancelled, call the adult who dropped off the child and the adult who will collect. The child stays with the escort in the supervised lounge. A child in this service is never booked into a hotel alone; the duty manager decides with the parents what happens next.

# Cancellation handling and rebooking priority

## Purpose

This procedure says who decides a cancellation at HUB, what is done in the first hour, and in which order passengers are rebooked.

## Deciding to cancel

Operations control decides a cancellation with the duty manager. The reason is recorded before any passenger is told.

## The first hour

1. Record the cancellation and its reason in the operations system.
2. Notify booked passengers once with the notify_passengers tool, reason "cancellation".
3. Open the transfer desk and the rebooking queue.
4. Start delay care under SOP-GND-014.

## Rebooking priority

Rebook in this order when seats are short.

| Priority | Passengers |
|---|---|
| 1 | Unaccompanied minors and passengers with reduced mobility |
| 2 | Passengers with onward connections in the next 6 hours |
| 3 | Families travelling with infants |
| 4 | All other passengers, in booking order |

## After the event

The duty manager writes a short report within 24 hours: the cause, the number of passengers, the care given and the vouchers issued.

# Unattended baggage and security alerts

## Purpose

This procedure covers unattended items found in ZZ areas at HUB. It is restricted to security staff and duty managers.

## First response

1. Do not touch or move the item.
2. Ask people nearby whether it belongs to them.
3. If nobody claims it within 2 minutes, call the security control room on the internal line and say "Code Amber" with the location.
4. Keep people at least 50 metres away until security officers arrive.

## Gate impact

Security control decides whether a gate is closed. The duty manager is told the expected closure time and moves departures if needed under SOP-GND-022.

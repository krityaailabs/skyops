# Crew duty limit extensions

## Purpose

This procedure covers what happens when a delay would take a ZZ crew past its planned duty limit.

## Extensions

The commander may extend the duty period by up to 2 hours for unforeseen circumstances that arise after reporting. Crew control records each extension and the reason.

| Situation | Who decides | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Extension up to 2 hours | Commander, with crew control | Extension report |
| Beyond 2 hours | Not allowed: replace the crew | Crew change log |

## Planning around limits

Crew control checks the duty limit against every new estimated departure. When the limit will be exceeded, crew control starts a crew change before asking for an extension.

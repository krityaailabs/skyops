# Assistance for passengers with reduced mobility

## Purpose

This procedure covers passengers who have asked for mobility assistance at HUB, from check-in to the aircraft door. Assistance is delivered by the contracted assistance team; ZZ agents request it, track it and keep the passenger informed.

## Assistance codes

Bookings carry a special service request code that tells the assistance team what the passenger needs.

| Code | What the passenger needs | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| WCHR | Can walk short distances and climb steps, needs help over long distances | Wheelchair to the aircraft door |
| WCHS | Can walk short distances, cannot climb steps | Wheelchair and ambulift or air bridge |
| WCHC | Cannot walk, needs help to the seat | Wheelchair, ambulift and aisle chair |
| MAAS | Needs meet-and-assist without a wheelchair | Escort |

## Before boarding

1. Check the special service request code on the booking against what the passenger tells you.
2. Request assistance in the ground operations portal at least 90 minutes before departure.
3. Tag the passenger's own wheelchair or mobility aid for delivery to the aircraft door.
4. Board passengers who need assistance first, and disembark them last unless they ask otherwise.

## During delays

When a flight with assisted passengers is delayed, tell the assistance team the new estimated departure as soon as it changes. Passengers must not be left in a wheelchair at the gate for more than 30 minutes without a check. Offer a seat in the assisted waiting area and make sure the passenger can reach toilets and refreshments. For powered wheelchairs, check the battery procedure in SOP-GND-031 again if the aircraft type changes.

## Hotel stays

If a delay leads to a hotel stay under SOP-GND-014, book an accessible room and accessible transport, and record the assistance code on the hotel booking.

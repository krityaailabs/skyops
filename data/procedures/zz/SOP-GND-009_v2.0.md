# Hotel vouchers for overnight delays

## Purpose

This procedure sets out when hub agents give ZZ passengers a hotel voucher because their departure has moved overnight.

## Eligibility

A passenger is eligible for a hotel room when the delay at the gate is 4 hours or more and the new departure is after midnight local time.

## Voucher limits

Agents may issue a hotel voucher of up to 250 EUR per room without approval. Above 250 EUR, call the duty manager. Rooms are booked through the contracted hotel list.

## Issuing the voucher

1. Check the passenger's booking and the new departure time.
2. Book the room at a contracted hotel.
3. Issue the hotel voucher, up to 250 EUR without approval.
4. Give the passenger the hotel name and the transport details.

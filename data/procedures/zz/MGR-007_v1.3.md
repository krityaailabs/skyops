# Goodwill and compensation authority limits

## Purpose

This procedure sets how much goodwill each role at HUB may offer a ZZ passenger after a disruption, in addition to the care in SOP-GND-014. It is for duty managers only. Do not share these limits with passengers or with agents.

## Authority limits

| Role | Goodwill per passenger | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Gate or transfer agent | None | Refer to the duty manager |
| Duty manager | Up to 400 EUR | Travel voucher or refund of extras |
| Station manager | Above 400 EUR | Any form |

## Applying goodwill

Goodwill is a decision about one passenger's circumstances, not a standard payment. Record the reason, the amount and the form against the booking. Never offer goodwill as a condition of the passenger withdrawing a complaint.

# Lithium batteries and mobility aids

## Purpose

This procedure tells check-in and ramp staff how to accept powered wheelchairs, mobility aids and spare lithium batteries on ZZ flights.

## Spare batteries and power banks

| Battery size | Carry-on | Checked bag |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 100 Wh | Allowed | Not allowed |
| Over 100 Wh up to 160 Wh | Allowed with ZZ approval, at most two spares | Not allowed |
| Over 160 Wh | Not allowed | Not allowed |

## Powered wheelchairs

1. Ask the passenger for the battery type and how to isolate it.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions to protect the battery from short circuits; if the battery can be removed, it travels in the cabin.
3. Tag the wheelchair and record the battery type on the loading message.
4. Tell the captain through the load control team.

## When the aircraft changes

If the aircraft type changes after acceptance, recheck the wheelchair's dimensions against the hold door and repeat the battery checks.

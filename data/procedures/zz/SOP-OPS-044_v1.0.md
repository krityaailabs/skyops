# Diversions: passenger care at the alternate airport

## Purpose

This procedure covers ZZ flights that land at an alternate airport instead of their destination.

## First actions

1. Operations control tells the duty manager the alternate airport and the expected time on the ground.
2. The duty manager contacts the handling agent at the alternate airport.
3. Passengers are told once, with the notify_passengers tool, reason "diversion".

## Care at the alternate airport

If passengers stay on board for more than 2 hours, the crew offers water and keeps the cabin ventilated. If they disembark, the handling agent provides meal vouchers on the same scale as SOP-GND-014, paid by ZZ. A hotel is arranged only if the onward flight is the next day.

## Onward travel

The duty manager chooses between continuing with the same aircraft, a replacement aircraft, or ground transport when the destination is near. Record the decision and its reason.
